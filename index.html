Notes & caveats

Full 10-item cutoff is ≥4/10. The 7-item interview-only variant (DN4-i, no examination) is commonly used with a cutoff of ≥3/7 — shown above as a subtotal.

A screen, not a diagnosis: reported sensitivity roughly 75–98% and specificity roughly 37–96% across populations; weaker for trigeminal neuralgia. Confirm against the IASP grading of neuropathic pain (history compatible with a lesion/disease of the somatosensory system; pain in a neuroanatomically plausible distribution; confirmatory test).

Notes & caveats

The 2016 revision raised the lower WPI bound to ≥4 (the alternative route is WPI 4–6 with SSS ≥9, not 3–6). It added the generalised-pain criterion to stop regional pain syndromes being misclassified.

Jaw (left/right), chest and abdomen contribute to the WPI total but do not, on their own, make a region count toward the 4-of-5 generalised-pain criterion.

A fibromyalgia diagnosis is valid irrespective of other diagnoses and does not require excluding other conditions. These are classification criteria, not a substitute for clinical reasoning about nociplastic mechanisms.
